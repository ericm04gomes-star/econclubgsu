About the club

Economics, discussed with the people who practice it

The Economics Club connects students at the Robinson College of Business and the Andrew Young School with faculty, alumni and professionals working in economics and related fields.

Purpose statement

The Economics Club exists to bring RCB and AYS students, academia, and professional resources together, concentrating on the importance of networking, professionalism and building valuable industry skills. The organization is a channel for talent acquisition between our members, employers and our alumni network, through events that discuss economics and important economic trends. Our goal is to create a strong community for all members.

What we aim to do

  • Connect students and faculty.
  • Promote economic knowledge and discussion.
  • Build a community for students interested in economics.
  • Explore academic, professional and research opportunities in economics.

Meeting formats

Two kinds of meeting, alternating Thursdays

Both are open to members and to students who have not yet joined.

Coffee chats

Each chat brings a guest from economics, finance, policy or business into an hour of open conversation with students. Past guests include the chair of the Economics department, faculty from several fields, and professionals from industry.

What to expect
  • A small group, with time set aside for questions
  • No preparation required
  • Conversation rather than formal presentation
  • Typically held midday, one hour

Learning events

On the Thursdays without a coffee chat, we host a larger learning-focused event. These have included program nights, research panels, policy simulations and a mixer with students from other Atlanta universities.

What to expect
  • Panels, workshops and member-led sessions
  • Structured activities rather than open conversation
  • Occasionally held jointly with other institutions
  • Typically 5:30 to 6:30 p.m.

Academic support

Learning & Tutoring Center

Coursework in economics is demanding, and the university maintains a dedicated resource for it. The Learning & Tutoring Center provides free tutoring to all Georgia State students, in person and through scheduled online sessions. We encourage members to use it early in the term rather than at the point of difficulty.

Visit the center

Attend one meeting before deciding.

Attendance does not require membership, and there are no dues.
