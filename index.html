Our purpose

A community for students interested in economics

The Economics Club exists to bring RCB and AYS students, academia, and professional resources together, concentrating on networking, professionalism and building valuable industry skills. The organization is a channel for talent acquisition between our members, employers and our alumni network.

More about the club
  • Connect students and faculty.
  • Promote economic knowledge and discussion.
  • Build a community for students interested in economics.
  • Explore academic, professional and research opportunities in economics.
