Common questions

Before you join

Do I need to be an economics major?

No. Membership is open to students of every major, and no economics coursework is expected. Members come from across the Robinson College of Business and the Andrew Young School.

Are there dues?

No. There are no dues at any level of membership, and no application to complete.

Do I have to come every week?

No. Come to the meetings that interest you. Attendance at a single meeting carries no further obligation.

Attend one meeting before deciding.

Attendance does not require membership, and there are no dues.
