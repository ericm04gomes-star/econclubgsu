Economics Review

The Georgia State Economics Review

A student journal publishing original work in economics. Currently in preparation.

Forthcoming

Two routes to publication

A faculty recommendation is not a prerequisite for consideration. Papers written for coursework are eligible, as is work developed independently.

Faculty recommendation

Faculty may put forward papers written for their own courses. The work is read by the people who taught the material, which means strong coursework reaches the editors without the student having to nominate themselves.

Direct submission

Students may submit their own work. If you have written something you would want others to read, you may send it in yourself, whether it was written for a course or developed independently.

Still being finalized

What is not decided yet

Submission deadlines, formatting requirements and the editorial review process are being settled. They will be published on this page and circulated in the GroupMe once confirmed.

Join the GroupMe for the announcement

What to do in the meantime

Students intending to submit should retain final copies of their coursework, together with any accompanying data or code.

Worth keeping
  • The final version of the paper, not a draft
  • Data files and any code used to produce results
  • The assignment prompt, where there was one
  • Any written feedback from the instructor

Attend one meeting before deciding.

Attendance does not require membership, and there are no dues.
