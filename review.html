Submissions will open in two ways

Faculty may recommend papers from their courses, and students may submit their own work directly.

Publication proceeds through two routes, and a faculty recommendation is not a prerequisite for consideration. Papers written for coursework are eligible, as is work developed independently.

Submission deadlines, formatting requirements and the editorial review process are being finalized. They will be published on this page and circulated in the GroupMe once confirmed.

Students intending to submit should retain final copies of their coursework, together with any accompanying data or code.

Attend one meeting before deciding.

Attendance does not require membership.
